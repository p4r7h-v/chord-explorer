How to play

Play: hold keys 1–7 or a chord pad. Release to stop. Try 1 → 6 → 3 → 7.

Shape: the last chord you played stays selected. H/L changes inversion; J/K changes octave. O spreads the notes. Each chord remembers its shape.

Hold: Space latches the selected chord so you can edit it hands-free. Esc stops everything.

Sound: P switches piano/supersaw. B toggles the low root. Click outside a dropdown before using shortcuts.

The trail remembers order, not timing. Reload resets this session. All audio is synthesized locally.
